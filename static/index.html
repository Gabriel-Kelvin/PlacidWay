YOUR INFORMATION

A clear view of what we store.

Previous questions and answers are held temporarily in server memory for this conversation, for up to 24 hours. New conversation clears that context. Conversation text is not saved as chat history. Topic counts, answer outcomes, and random chat identifiers may be kept for up to 30 days for quality reporting.

Questions are sent to Groq for processing. Basic email, phone, and key patterns are redacted first, but automated redaction is imperfect. Please don’t enter medical records or sensitive personal information.

This app does not collect names, emails, or quote requests. Topic statistics and public source content are synchronized to the protected Supabase backend.

This is an assessment build. Use PlacidWay’s official quote form to contact their team.

English evidence remains visible for translated answers. Citations support source claims; they do not establish medical effectiveness.
