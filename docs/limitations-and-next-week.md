# Known limits and the next week of work

This assessment prioritizes auditable evidence over conversational breadth. English factual responses are deliberately extractive. Replies are capped at eight content lines; those lines wrap with screen width. Extractive wording can be less natural than a generated summary. Every displayed source quote is checked against the current stored snapshot, and price tokens must occur in those exact passages. These checks prevent invented numbers, but do not prove that a publisher's medical claims are correct or that a selected passage fully answers every possible question.

Groq handles uncertain routing, evidence selection and translation. Translation checks preserve numbers, but meaning still needs native-speaker review, especially medical wording and right-to-left languages. Some unusual wording may be refused unnecessarily. The evaluation report records actual passes and failures and is not a claim of perfect safety or a clinical validation.

The knowledge base is exactly seven pages. It excludes linked recommendations, related articles, and video transcripts. Recovery time is not equated with treatment duration. Clinic prices may be dated or internally inconsistent; the assistant quotes their context and recommends a personalized quote. Page structure changes can require an extractor update. A failed refresh preserves the last good content, but cannot make it current.

Supabase is the persistent backend for topic statistics, errors, source snapshots and shared atomic rate limits. No SQLite database is used. Current-chat turns stay only in process memory, clear on New conversation and are lost on restart or an idle-host cold start. The volatile metric retry buffer may lose pending records during a restart in a database outage. Free hosting can sleep and pauses background maintenance. HTTPS and secure cookies are enabled in deployment. The shared admin password is suitable for this assessment; staff accounts, MFA, database-native retention scheduling and monitoring remain improvements. STT/TTS are not implemented.

Lead capture and contact storage have been removed at the user’s request. Quote and booking requests point to PlacidWay’s official form. The app does not collect names or emails. Questions and recent context receive basic automated redaction before provider transmission, not guaranteed anonymization. Users are told not to submit sensitive health information. The admin toolbar contains only Refresh and Sign out, with unanswered topics visible and no exports. Supabase automatically synchronizes topic summaries and public source content.

With another week, I would add professionally reviewed multilingual templates, independent semantic adversarial evaluations, stricter structured price records with automated scope checks, a human source-change review queue, and better evidence ranking for long questions. I would add Supabase Auth roles and audit trails, shared limits and retention jobs, test restore procedures, and then deploy with uptime monitoring for the required seven-day period. Deployment and the public demo are intentionally deferred at the user's request; seven-day uptime has not been demonstrated.

Current-chat memory is intentionally ephemeral and single-process. Multiple workers would need a shared temporary store with deletion and expiry semantics. Supabase is not required for the local conversation feature. Admin analytics retain topic summaries rather than user question text.
