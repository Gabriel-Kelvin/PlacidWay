AI Automation Assessment: Website Knowledge Chatbot
Oct 7, 2026 · @PW Dev
Overview
Build a chatbot that answers visitor questions using only content from the PlacidWay pages we share with you.
This assessment is for the AI Automation Engineer role. We want to see how you collect web content, ground an LLM in it, and stop it from inventing answers. A small, reliable bot beats a large, impressive-looking one that makes things up.
Context
PlacidWay is a medical tourism platform. Patients compare treatments, clinics, destinations and costs, then contact us for quotes. Many of their questions are already answered on our pages, but they are spread across treatment, cost, destination and clinic pages.
The bot should answer questions like "How much does a dental implant cost in Mexico?" in seconds, using our own content. It should always point the patient to the page the answer came from.

Requirements
You may use any language, framework, LLM provider or no-code tool (Python, Node, n8n, LangChain, LlamaIndex, OpenAI, Claude, open-source models). Tell us what you chose and why.
Must have
Content ingestion. The bot reads its content from the shared URLs.
Knowledge base. The bot finds the right content for each question.
Grounded answers. The bot answers only from the website content. Each answer cites its source URL(s).
Honest "I don't know". If the answer is not in the content, the bot says so and suggests contacting PlacidWay for a free quote. It never invents prices, clinics or medical facts.
Cost questions. Prices are returned with currency and any context the page gives (e.g. "starting from", package includes).
Simple chat UI. A simple chat interface where we can type questions.
Refresh. A way to update the bot's content when the pages change.
Live deployment. The bot is available on a public URL we can open and test without installing anything. Keep it running for at least 7 days after submission.
Nice to have
Answers in the user's language (e.g. Spanish or Arabic questions about English pages)
Handles follow-up questions using chat history ("what about in Turkey?")
A short medical disclaimer when relevant, without repeating it on every answer
Lead capture: offers to collect name and email when the user wants a quote
Logging of questions the bot could not answer, so we can find content gaps

Rules and FAQ
Can I use AI coding assistants? Yes. You must understand and be able to explain every part of your code.
Which LLM should I use? Any. Free tiers or local models are fine. We do not reimburse API costs.
Can I use a hosted chatbot builder? Yes, as long as the ingestion, grounding and fallback logic are visible and explained.
What if a page has no price? The bot should say the price is not listed and suggest requesting a quote.
Who owns the code? You do. We use it only to evaluate this application.
